Cacao 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

Cacao 是家庭零用錢管理 App。我們的原則是：你的資料只屬於你。

我們收集什麼

不收集。Cacao 沒有伺服器、沒有帳號系統、沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具。我們（開發者）看不到你的任何資料。

資料存在哪裡

  • 孩子名稱、收支紀錄、目標、任務等資料，只存在你手機上的 App 資料庫。
  • 父母驗證碼（PIN）與孩子密碼只以雜湊值存在手機上，不會離開手機。

Google 雲端硬碟備份（選用）

  • 只有你在 App 設定中主動開啟時才會使用。
  • 備份檔存在你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟中 App 專屬的隱藏資料夾（appDataFolder），只有 Cacao 能讀寫，開發者無法存取。
  • Cacao 只要求 drive.appdata 權限，看不到你雲端硬碟的其他檔案。
  • 你可以隨時在 App 關閉備份，或在 Google 帳戶設定中移除 Cacao 的存取權並刪除備份。

匯出備份檔

你可以把資料匯出成檔案，存到你選擇的位置；可選擇加上密碼。檔案去哪裡由你決定。

兒童

Cacao 讓父母與孩子一起使用，不向任何人（包括兒童）收集個人資料，也不含廣告。

刪除資料

解除安裝 App 就會刪除手機上的所有資料。雲端備份請在 App 內或 Google 帳戶設定中刪除。

Cacao Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Cacao is a family allowance app. Our principle: your data belongs to you.

What we collect

Nothing. Cacao has no servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. We (the developer) cannot see any of your data.

Where your data lives

  • Children's names, records, goals and tasks are stored only in the app's database on your phone.
  • Parent PINs and child passwords are stored only as hashes on your phone and never leave it.

Google Drive backup (optional)

  • Used only if you turn it on in Settings.
  • Backups are stored in your own Google Drive, in a hidden app-specific folder (appDataFolder) that only Cacao can access. The developer cannot access it.
  • Cacao requests only the drive.appdata scope and cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • You can turn backup off at any time, or remove Cacao's access and delete its backups in your Google Account settings.

Exported backup files

You can export your data to a file, optionally protected by a password, and save it wherever you choose.

Children

Cacao is designed for parents and children to use together. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children, and contains no ads.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data on your phone. Cloud backups can be deleted in the app or in your Google Account settings.
