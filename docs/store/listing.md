## 繁體中文（zh-TW）

**App 名稱**（30 字內）：Cacao 零用錢

**簡短說明**（80 字內）：
孩子的第一個存錢筒：存款、錢包、目標、任務一目了然。全家一起用，離線、不用註冊。

**完整說明**：

Cacao 讓爸爸媽媽和孩子一起管理零用錢，從小練習存錢與規劃。

▍存款與錢包
・存款由父母保管，錢包是孩子手上的錢
・常態零用錢自動定期入帳，可暫停、可調整金額
・每一筆收入與支出都留下紀錄

▍儲蓄目標
・孩子自己設定想買的東西，存款會依序填進目標
・達成後確認鎖錢，買到時再從目標出金

▍任務獎勵
・父母（也可以代爺爺奶奶）出任務、設定獎勵
・孩子完成後，父母當場驗收、獎勵自動入帳

▍成長紀錄
・每年存了多少、花了多少，一年一年看得見

▍安心使用
・全家共用一台手機，父母模式用驗證碼保護
・完全離線，不用註冊帳號，沒有廣告
・資料只存在你的手機；可選擇備份到你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟，或匯出成備份檔
・4 種可愛主題，繁體中文與英文

## English (en-US)

**App name**: Cacao Allowance

**Short description** (≤80 chars):
Your child's first piggy bank: savings, wallet, goals and tasks. Offline.

**Full description**:

Cacao helps parents and kids manage allowance together, building saving and planning habits early.

▍Savings & wallet
• Savings are held by parents; the wallet is the money in your child's hands
• Recurring allowance is added automatically — pause or change it anytime
• Every income and expense is recorded

▍Savings goals
• Kids set goals for things they want; savings fill goals in order
• Lock the money when a goal is reached, then pay out when it's time to buy

▍Task rewards
• Parents (or on behalf of grandparents) create tasks with rewards
• When a child finishes, a parent verifies on the spot and the reward is added

▍Growth
• See how much was saved and spent, year by year

▍Peace of mind
• One shared family phone; parent mode protected by a PIN
• Fully offline, no sign-up, no ads
• Data stays on your phone; optional backup to your own Google Drive or to an export file
• 4 cute themes, Traditional Chinese and English
